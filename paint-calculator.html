Related Questions

Before you start painting

How much paint do I need for my project?

Coverage varies by product — most wall coatings cover 8–10 m²/L. Multiply your surface area by the coats needed, then divide by the coverage rate.

How long before I can apply a second coat?

Drying times range from 4 hours up to 16 hours depending on the product. Always check the specific product before recoating.

What does the 10-year guarantee cover?

Our guarantee reflects confidence in the durability and performance of Parker Paints products when applied according to the recommended surface preparation, primer, and application method.
