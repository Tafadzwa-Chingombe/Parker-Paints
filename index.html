The Challenge

Zimbabwe’s climate is tough on surfaces.

Harsh sunlight, heavy rains, and everyday wear can quickly fade colours, crack finishes, and damage poorly protected walls.

Intense Sunlight

UV rays cause fading, chalking, and breakdown of exterior surfaces.

Rain & Moisture

Persistent rain and humidity seep into walls, leading to stains and damage.

Cracking & Peeling

Low quality paints can’t expand and contract, causing cracks and peeling.

The Solution

Paints engineered for durability and beauty

Parker Paints combines innovation and quality materials to deliver finishes that last longer and look better.

UV Resistant

Engineered with advanced UV-blocking technology.

Weatherproof

It actively repels moisture, preventing blistering, peeling, and mold growth before they can start.

Long-lasting Colour

It resists the typical dulling effects of time and environment, keeping its “just-painted” depth and brilliance for years to come.

Easy Application

Designed for a smooth, buttery glide, our paint features exceptional self-leveling properties that minimize brush marks and roller tracks.

Built to withstand Zimbabwe’s toughest conditions.
