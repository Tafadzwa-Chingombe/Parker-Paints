About Parker Paints

Made to international standards.

Our products exceed the standard required for many quality hallmarks awarded by authorities in various parts of the world.

Est. January 2021

Parker Paints (PVT) LTD was established as a fully-fledged Zimbabwean paint manufacturer of decorative, protective, and industrial coatings.

We don’t just make paints — we manufacture high-performance coatings that combine durability, consistency and colour precision, backed by decades of industry experience.
